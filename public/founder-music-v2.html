The Music Profile · Where the Research Comes From

A voice trained in the old way

Every metric above stands on four decades of musicianship. Before the models and the papers, there was a boy in Kerala learning sargams the way they have been passed down for centuries — voice to voice, guru to student.

  1. Kerala · Age 10

    Carnatic training under a legend

    Formal Carnatic training from age ten under his late guru, Oachira Balakrishnan Sir — former Principal of RLV College of Music and Fine Arts and a revered figure in Kerala’s Carnatic tradition. Prize-winning performances followed throughout his school years.

  2. Engineering years · Coimbatore

    College lead singer

    Lead singer of his engineering college, taking first prize and a cash award for his rendition of Sonu Nigam’s “Tu” — among many competition wins across school and college.

  3. Dubai · 2000s

    A hundred stages in the Gulf

    While studying Hindustani music for several years in Dubai, he sang at roughly 100 functions — including programs featuring K. J. Yesudas, K. S. Chithra, Vijay Yesudas, and playback singer and music director M. G. Sreekumar.

  4. Kerala

    His own band

    Back home, he led his own band, performing regularly at weddings and events across Kerala.

  5. United States · Present

    Singing on American stages

    For the past two years he has performed with a band of vocalists and musicians in the U.S., through cultural associations such as AMMA and GAMMA — including audition-based programs — and sang a Tamil devotional piece for a major Swami Ayyappa organization.

  6. Atlanta, GA · 2026

    Founded the Music Cafe band

    His own band, founded in Atlanta — live sets with a rotating circle of vocalists and musicians. Watch the latest band reel →

  7. Aug 2026 · Atlanta

    Selected to sing with Mano — live

    Chosen through audition to perform alongside legendary playback singer Mano at his live concert in Atlanta, August 2026.
